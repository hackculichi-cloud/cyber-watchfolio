Visitors can scan the portfolio and show real work without reading a résumé in every section. Brief titles, status and summaries remain visible; the full technical record stays available through native disclosures. Existing CVs and original uploads are unchanged because no training or employment facts changed.

- Customer-service experience leads with employers, roles and dates; full responsibilities remain expandable. About retains the complete biography behind a clear action. Barbering retains its school and honest collection states while the future haircut workflow is optional.

CV pages remain the full-document destination.
