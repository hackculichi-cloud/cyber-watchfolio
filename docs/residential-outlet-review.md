## Assessment and missing documentation

1. **Isolation was omitted, not merely undocumented.** Energized replacement is not presented as an acceptable procedure. If a client refuses isolation, postpone the replacement. Future documentation should record circuit identification, disconnection, prevention of reconnection and absence-of-voltage verification, including instrument checks on a known live source before and after.
2. **Protection/device compatibility needs review before further use.** A 15 A receptacle must not be assumed appropriate on a 30 A branch circuit. A qualified electrician should identify the circuit, conductor gauge/material/ampacity, breaker suitability and device rating, and correct the arrangement as required. A photograph cannot establish those specifications; do not prescribe a replacement breaker based on the image alone.
3. **Protective earth was checked according to the owner.** The method and measured result have not been recorded. Colors and a three-light tester cannot prove earth continuity/impedance or exclude a bootleg ground. Green conductors must be reserved for protective earth; verify any required metal-box bonding. Do not infer a fault solely from two green conductors sharing a terminal.
4. **Terminals and insulation need close inspection.** Verify the manufacturer's permitted conductor number, size/material, stripping length and torque. Close-ups show conductor ends and marked/discolored insulation, but cannot establish clamping force, insulation damage, overheating or whether taped areas contain joints. Any joints must use suitable approved connections inside the box; tape alone is not a mechanical connector.
5. **Document final checks.** Record measured points and results for polarity, live-neutral/live-earth/neutral-earth as appropriate, effective earth and required protective-device operation. Resistance/continuity tests must be performed de-energized. A nominal voltage reading and illuminated plug-in tester alone do not certify installation safety.

This is a review of the submitted record, not an inspection or declaration of compliance. No completion dates, customer identity, circuit gauge, earth measurement, tightening torque or regulatory certification were invented.
