# Direct Home with fixed area links

Home opens directly, without the electrical welcome presentation or its replay button. The welcome component, styles, artwork and exclusive translations have been removed.

The seven professional area links stay at fixed coordinates. The rotation, counter-rotation and play/pause control are removed. The tool-name legend and its reserved space below the fox are also removed. Area names remain visible, and hovering or focusing an area still previews its outfit and three distinct, color-matched tool images. All seven links remain reachable on phones.

Browser coverage includes 320/390/768/1440 px, both motion preferences, first and returning visits, stable coordinates, absent welcome artwork requests, navigation, keyboard focus, floating tool colors and clearance, and unchanged training access.
